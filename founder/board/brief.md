# Board brief: Self-Recharging Washing Machine (working name)

## The idea, in the founder's words (lightly cleaned up)

A washing machine whose drum (the founder calls it the "turbine") rotates the clothes.
Inside the machine are two 15,000 mAh batteries.

1. Battery A starts full; Battery B starts empty.
2. Battery A powers the motor that spins the drum (electrical energy -> mechanical energy).
3. The spinning drum also turns a generator (mechanical energy -> electrical energy), which charges Battery B.
4. When Battery A is empty and Battery B is full, the roles swap and the cycle repeats.

The founder describes it as "energy travels from the full battery, through the turbine, into the empty battery",
and sees it as a good concept for electricity. They want to know:
- Is this legit?
- How could it be made bigger (scaled up)?

## What is known
- Battery size: 2 x 15,000 mAh (voltage not specified).
- Product: a washing machine (the drum does the washing while the energy loops).

## What is NOT known yet
- Target customer, price, sales channel, budget, team, prototype status. None were given.

## Note for board members
Score the business, not the founder. If a fact is missing, name it as an open question
rather than inventing it. If you need to check the engineering, use basic physics
(energy conservation, typical motor/generator/battery efficiencies, energy a washing
machine actually uses per cycle) and show your arithmetic.
