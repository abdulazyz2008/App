# Board brief: CrashEcho AI + RoadMemory

This brief is everything the board knows. It is taken from the founders' 6-slide hackathon pitch deck and their answers to three follow-up questions. Nothing has been added beyond that.

## The idea file

- What it is: a background phone app for two-wheeler riders that detects crashes (and sends SOS with live location and medical ID to two family contacts, plus the nearest suitable hospital and a one-tap ambulance call), and also detects potholes, skids and near misses, saves them anonymously, and warns the next rider at that exact spot.
- Who it is for: two-wheeler riders in India, first of all delivery riders. Secondary beneficiaries: families, paramedics, other riders, city road authorities.
- What it sells, at what price: NOT DECIDED. The founders asked for a suggested model. Candidate models (hypotheses only): (a) rider freemium subscription; (b) B2B per-rider fee to delivery fleets / gig platforms; (c) insurers paying for crash alerts and risk data; (d) city / government bodies paying for road-hazard data. No prices set.
- Where and how: one Indian metro first (city not chosen); Android + iPhone app; on-device sensors in the background.
- Budget and constraints: team of four (Aman, Safi, Azeem, Tanay), hackathon stage, planning to raise money next. No funding, users or revenue. Budget not stated.

## What the pitch deck says (slide by slide)

1. Title: "CrashEcho AI + RoadMemory. Detect the danger. Remember the road. Protect the next rider. Reach help faster." Team Innovventure.
2. Problem (story): Rahul, a delivery rider, skids on a water-filled pothole on a rainy night and rides on; nobody stops to file a complaint. 20 minutes later another rider hits the same pothole, falls, and lies unconscious; his family doesn't know, helpers don't know his blood group, other riders keep arriving without warning. "Problem: dangers nobody records. Help comes late. The next rider gets no warning."
3. Solution: runs in the background on any Android or iPhone; the app doesn't need to be open. An on-device "AI chip" model reads accelerometer, gyroscope and GPS+speed and classifies pothole/skid, hard brake, and crash.
   - CrashEcho: "Are you okay?" prompt for 10 seconds; if no reply, SOS on the rider's phone and on 2 loved ones' phones with live location, medical ID (name, blood group, conditions, allergies, family numbers), the nearest trauma centre, and a one-tap ambulance (108) call.
   - RoadMemory: potholes, skids and near misses saved anonymously; once confirmed by several riders, others are warned ("Pothole ahead, slow down"). Accident sites become "red zones" for riders behind.
4. Differentiation table vs maps apps, phone crash detection, and pothole apps: claims only CrashEcho does all of: automatic background detection, near misses and skids, warning the next rider at the exact spot, SOS on 2 loved ones' phones, hospital matched to injury, red zone for riders behind, any phone built for two-wheelers. Chain: Detect -> Verify -> Remember -> Warn next rider -> Alert family -> Medical ID -> Right hospital.
5. "Doesn't Apple already do this?": Apple Crash Detection is on newer iPhones/Watches, built mainly for car crashes, notifies contacts with location; Apple Maps hazards need manual reports. CrashEcho claims any phone, tuned for two-wheelers, catches near misses before crashes, warns the next rider, matches the right hospital, alerts riders behind.
6. Benefits: riders (warnings, faster help), families (instant SOS), paramedics (blood group/allergies on screen), other riders (red zones, pothole alerts), cities (data on which roads to repair first). Privacy: only event type and location shared, never name or trip.
