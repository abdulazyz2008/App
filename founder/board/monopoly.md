# Monopoly lens
Score: 1/10 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- A washer that does not need the grid is a real need. It could become a narrow, ownable niche: battery or solar washing for off-grid homes.
- The founder is thinking in terms of energy flow, which is the right place to start.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **The core mechanism cannot work. It is not a breakthrough.** Energy is conserved, so B can never receive more than A gave out, and every step loses energy as heat (second law). Typical efficiencies are motor ~90%, generator ~90%, and charging ~95%. That makes one loop 0.9 × 0.9 × 0.95 ≈ **77%**, even before any washing is done. The washing itself is energy leaving the loop as friction and heat in the water and clothes. The generator only adds drag on the motor. Each swap delivers less than the one before: 100% → 77% → 59% → 46%. With the generator removed, the same battery washes for longer.
2. **The batteries are tiny compared with the job.** The voltage was not given. At 3.7 V (a typical Li-ion cell), 15 Ah × 3.7 V ≈ **55.5 Wh**. The drum motor plausibly needs ~100–300 Wh per cycle, and ~0.5–1.5 kWh once water is heated. These are rough ranges, and the founder should measure them. On those figures one full battery covers about 20–55% of the motor's energy and about 4–11% of a heated cycle.
3. **There is no proprietary core, so there is no path to a monopoly.** Without the loop, what remains is an ordinary battery washer. It has no 10x advantage and nothing that stops a copycat.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What voltage are the batteries? How many Wh does one full cycle actually draw on a meter?
- Has a prototype ever ended with B holding more energy than A started with?
- Is the real goal off-grid washing? If so, who is the first small market, and how do the first 100 customers arrive?
- Who already sells battery, solar or hand-powered washers to that buyer? (Open question; no market data was given.)
- What is the secret: what do you know that others do not?
- Timing, team, price, channel? None were given.
