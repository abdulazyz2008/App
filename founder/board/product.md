# Product lens
Score: 2 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- The customer moment is easy to picture: start a load and never think about the power bill or the socket. The Product lens rewards an idea that can be said in one sentence, and this one can.
- The founder wants laundry that does not depend on the grid. That need may be real, but the mechanism described here cannot meet it.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **The loop makes no energy.** Work back from the customer's load. Battery B can only get what the generator gets after the clothes have taken their share, and every step loses some. With typical efficiencies (motor 85%, generator 85%, battery charging 95%), one swap returns 0.85 x 0.85 x 0.95 = about 69% of what left Battery A, and less if the drum does any washing. The batteries get emptier with every swap. Adding the generator also brakes the drum, so this machine does less washing than one with the generator taken out.
2. **The batteries are far too small for one load.** The voltage is not given. At 3.7 V (one lithium cell), 15 Ah x 3.7 V = 55.5 Wh per battery. At 12 V it is 180 Wh. The heating step needs about 15 L x 4.186 kJ/kg·K x 25 K (15 to 40 °C) = 1.57 MJ = 0.44 kWh. Assume the drum motor uses about 0.2 kWh more, for a total of about 0.64 kWh. Battery A covers about 9% of one load at 3.7 V, or about 28% at 12 V. The generator does nothing for the water heating, which needs the most energy.
3. **Nothing clear is left after you cut the loop.** Take out the impossible part and what remains is a washer with a battery inside. That battery still has to be charged from the grid or from solar, and the brief does not yet say who that product is for.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What is the battery voltage, and has a prototype run a full load? What did it measure?
- What should the first five minutes feel like: no socket, a lower bill, or use in an off-grid home?
- Where would the charging energy really come from: a wall socket, solar, or off-peak power?
- Who is the target customer, at what price, through which sales channel, and with what team and budget? None of these were given.
