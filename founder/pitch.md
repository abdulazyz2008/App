UniDash: a phone app that puts every official deadline, exam notice and announcement your students need into one feed, with alerts, so fewer of them miss submissions and exams.
Free for students. The university pays ₹300 per student per year (a 3,000-student college pays ₹9 lakh a year).
It connects to your existing Microsoft 365 and ERP/LMS once your IT team approves access, and reads what staff already publish, so nobody posts twice. Includes read receipts on announcements and a monthly report on missed deadlines.
Opening offer: a free one-term pilot with one batch, ending in a before-and-after report on missed submissions.
Built by a small Hyderabad startup; comes with a data-processing agreement under India's DPDP Act.
All prices are in Indian rupees. Give your price answers in ₹ per student per year.
