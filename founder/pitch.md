A 7 kg semi-automatic washing machine with a built-in lithium battery.
When the power goes out mid-wash, it switches to the battery and finishes the load (about 2 cold-water loads per full charge).
It charges from your normal plug when power is on, or from an optional 300 W solar panel.
Price: ₹21,999 for the machine, or ₹1,999 a month for 12 months on EMI. Solar panel kit: ₹6,500 extra.
Sold and installed by your local electronics dealer; 2-year warranty on the machine, 3 years on the battery, with service at the dealer.
