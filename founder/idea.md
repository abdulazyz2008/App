# CrashEcho AI + RoadMemory

- What it is: a background phone app for two-wheeler riders that detects crashes (and sends SOS with live location and medical ID to two family contacts, plus the nearest suitable hospital and a one-tap ambulance call), and also detects potholes, skids and near misses, saves them anonymously, and warns the next rider at that exact spot.
- Who it is for: two-wheeler riders in India, first of all delivery riders (gig workers riding long hours, at night, in rain); secondary beneficiaries are their families, paramedics, other riders and city road authorities.
- What it sells, at what price: NOT DECIDED. The founders asked the board to suggest a model. Candidate models (hypotheses, not decisions): (a) rider freemium subscription; (b) B2B per-rider fee to delivery fleets / gig platforms; (c) insurers paying for crash alerts and risk data; (d) city / government bodies paying for road-hazard data. No prices set.
- Where and how: launch in one Indian metro with dense two-wheeler traffic (city not yet chosen); distributed as an Android + iPhone app; runs on-device sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, GPS) in the background.
- Budget and constraints: hackathon project by a team of four (Team Innovventure: Aman, Safi, Azeem, Tanay); planning to raise money after the hackathon. No funding, users or revenue yet. Budget not stated.
