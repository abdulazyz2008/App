# UniDash (working name, not final)

- What it is: A mobile app that pulls everything a university student needs (marks, CGPA, upcoming university events, subjects, assignments with due dates, course materials, and Outlook / Microsoft 365 email) into one dashboard.
- Who it is for: University and college students, starting in India, later the US. Second buyer: the universities themselves (bulk licence).
- What it sells, at what price: Unified student dashboard app. Pricing undecided: about ₹100 per student per month if sold to students directly, or a bulk licence paid by the university.
- Where and how: India first, then the US. Delivered as a mobile app that connects to Outlook / Microsoft 365 and as many other student tools as possible.
- Budget and constraints: Not built yet. No budget, team size or timeline given. Founder is a first-year B.Tech student in Hyderabad.
