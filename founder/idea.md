# Self-Recharging Washing Machine (working name)

- What it is: a washing machine with two 15,000 mAh batteries; one battery drives the drum motor, and the spinning drum drives a generator that recharges the second battery; when the first runs empty the roles swap.
- Who it is for: not given yet (presumably households that want a washer that does not draw from the grid).
- What it sells, at what price: the washing machine itself; price not given yet.
