## What would flip a no

- If the dealer let me pay small amounts after harvest instead of a fixed EMI every month, and showed me it cleaning our muddy work clothes in front of me.
- If the price came down close to an ordinary semi-automatic, around ₹12,000-13,000, with a lower EMI like ₹800-1,000 a month, and a neighbour showed me it cleans muddy clothes properly.
- If it cost close to an ordinary semi-automatic, around thirteen or fourteen thousand, with the battery included.
- If my wife or mother asked for one, or I saw a neighbour's machine save real time and labour for a full year without trouble at the dealer.
- If the dealer brought the EMI down to around ₹1,300-1,400 a month, or threw in the solar panel free, so the gap with a normal machine felt small.
- A Diwali or harvest-season offer that brings it near ₹15,000 with a free gift, EMI I can pay after harvest instead of every month, and a neighbour I know already using it without trouble.
- If it cost under ₹10,000, or the monthly payment was around ₹500, and I saw a neighbour's muddy field clothes come out clean from it.
- If my cousin or the dealer I trust showed me one running in a house like ours with the same water problem, and the EMI came down to around ₹1,000 a month after the wedding expenses are done.
- A big Diwali or harvest-season offer that brings the price near ₹12,000 with a free gift, on a small EMI I can pay after the crop is sold.
- A relative or neighbour using one for a few months without trouble, and the dealer telling me in writing what a replacement battery costs.
- If a big brand I know from TV sold it, with the solar panel included, for under ₹12,000 or about ₹800 a month.
- If the full price with the solar panel came close to a normal semi-automatic, around ₹10,000-12,000, or the EMI dropped to a few hundred rupees a month.
