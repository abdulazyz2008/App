How it works
01 · VALIDATE
Catch bad data
Missing or junk names, broken emails, duplicates. Typos like gmial.com are fixed for you.
02 · DESIGN
Rules in plain English
“Winners get gold, others participation” sets up templates and emails.
03 · SIGN & SEAL
Provable PDFs
Each PDF is Ed25519-signed with a QR code. The whole batch is sealed with a Merkle root.
04 · DELIVER
Mapping Lock
Each attachment is re-checked against its recipient before sending. A wrong file can’t go out.

How anyone can check a certificate
1
Scan the QR
Opens the public page: “Verified, issued to X for Y”. A fake ID shows “Not verified”.
2
Upload the PDF
Its SHA-256 must match the signed original. Editing even one character fails.
3
Check the issuer key
Signatures are Ed25519. The public key is published, so anyone can verify independently.
